# Tenye Home & Fragrance (@tenyescents) captions

## 01. Your room can smell good without doing too much

You do not need five different fragrances fighting for attention. A lovely room does not have to smell overwhelming.

## 02. Where should you put your reed diffuser?

You buy a diffuser, tuck it into a hidden corner and wonder why you barely notice it. The right spot can make a simple difference.

## 03. Your home does not have to be perfect to feel cosy

There may be laundry waiting and dishes you have not finished. Sometimes a small reset is enough.

## 04. Why does the same scent smell different in two rooms?

You love a fragrance in the bedroom but barely notice it in the sitting room. The fragrance has not necessarily changed. The space might have.

## 05. How to choose a scent when shopping online

You cannot smell through your phone, so descriptions matter. Start with what you know you like, then explore.

## 06. A scent for your slow evening

You have finally finished the day and want your room to feel a little calmer. It is less about a perfect routine and more about enjoying your space.

## 07. Do not judge a fragrance in the first minute

You open a product and immediately decide whether you love it. Fragrance is an experience, not a race.

## 08. The entrance to your home matters

It is the first space you and your guests walk into. A warm welcome can begin before anyone says hello.

## 09. Do you prefer fresh or cosy scents?

Some days you want a room that feels airy and clean. Other days you want something warm and comforting. Your fragrance choices can change with your mood.

## 10. How to make your guest bathroom feel welcoming

You do not need to redesign the whole bathroom before visitors arrive. Small details can make people feel considered.

## 11. Your work corner deserves attention too

You spend hours at that desk, yet it is often the last place you think about. Make room for comfort where you spend your time.

## 12. Fragrance is personal

A scent your friend loves might not be your favourite. Your home should smell like a place you enjoy being.

## 13. A thoughtful housewarming gift does not have to be complicated

You want to take something useful but do not know the person's exact taste. A thoughtful gift starts with the person receiving it.

## 14. Keep your diffuser away from these spots

You love the bottle and want to place it right beside the window. A beautiful setup should also be a safe one.

## 15. A little Sunday home reset

You do not have to spend the entire Sunday deep-cleaning to feel ready for the week. A few thoughtful minutes can make home feel welcoming again.
