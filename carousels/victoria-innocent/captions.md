# Victoria Innocent (@thevictoriainnocent) captions

## 01. I am learning not to explain every decision

There was a time I felt I needed everyone to understand why I made a choice. Not every decision needs a full presentation.

## 02. Some days I am productive. Some days I am just tired

I can plan a beautiful week on Sunday and still have a completely different Tuesday. Consistency can include adjusting the plan.

## 03. Building something while working full-time is a lot

There are ideas in my notes, things I want to launch and work waiting for me too. Small progress still counts when your plate is full.

## 04. I have stopped waiting for everything to be perfect

I can spend so much time planning a project that I almost forget to start it. Sometimes starting teaches you what planning cannot.

## 05. I like having ideas. Execution is another story

My brain can give me five new business ideas before breakfast. An idea is fun. Building it is the actual work.

## 06. I am learning to keep work at work

It is easy to carry one awkward conversation into the rest of your evening. My whole life cannot revolve around one workday.

## 07. Nobody tells you how many tabs adulthood opens

There is work, bills, family, goals, messages and that one thing you keep forgetting to do. Being an adult seems to involve a lot of remembering.

## 08. I am trying to enjoy the process too

I can get so focused on what is next that I barely notice what is happening now. I want to remember the journey, not only the results.

## 09. Sometimes the best plan is to mind your business

Not in a rude way. Just in the sense of focusing on what you can actually control. Peace can look like fewer unnecessary explanations.

## 10. I am not interested in looking busy anymore

There is something about having a packed calendar that makes you feel important. I would rather have results than a dramatic to-do list.

## 11. I have learned that money needs attention

It is possible to work hard for money and still avoid looking closely at your spending. Money conversations should be part of everyday life.

## 12. I am allowed to change my mind

Sometimes an idea made sense six months ago and no longer fits where I am now. Growth sometimes looks like changing the plan.

## 13. My notes app knows too much

Business ideas, random thoughts, captions, reminders and things I want to buy are all living there. A note is not a project until something happens with it.

## 14. You can be ambitious and still need a quiet day

I want to do a lot with my life. That part has not changed. Ambition and enjoyment can exist in the same life.

## 15. I want to look back and know I actually tried

There are so many things I am curious about building and learning. I would rather learn from trying than keep wondering.
