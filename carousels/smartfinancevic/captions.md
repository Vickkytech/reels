# SmartFinanceVic (@smartfinancevic) captions

## 01. Your salary came in. Where did it go?

You were paid on Friday. By Wednesday, you are already checking your balance. Before you decide you need more money, find out what happened to the last one.

## 02. The Money Chronicles: she saved nothing for six months

Every month she promised herself she would save whatever was left. A small amount saved consistently is still a decision worth taking.

## 03. You can earn more and still feel broke

A salary increase sounds like relief. Then the subscriptions, outings and expenses grow too. Enjoy your money. Just do not let every increase disappear without a trace.

## 04. Before you invest, ask this question

Someone says an investment pays a very attractive return every month. If you cannot explain what you are investing in, slow down.

## 05. Your emergency fund is not boring

Nobody posts exciting pictures of money sitting quietly in an account. Not every naira needs to chase a return. Some money is there to give you breathing room.

## 06. The Money Chronicles: lending money you needed

He wanted to help a friend, so he lent out the money meant for his rent. Generosity is good. Putting yourself in trouble to prove it is another matter.

## 07. Saving and investing are not the same thing

You have money for school fees due in two months and money for a goal five years away. The right place for your money depends on when you need it.

## 08. You do not need to start investing with millions

Some people keep postponing investing because they think their money is too small. Start with knowledge, not pressure to look wealthy.

## 09. The Money Chronicles: the December spending hangover

December was fun. January arrived with school fees, rent and a very quiet account. You can enjoy the season without borrowing stress from the new year.

## 10. Why your budget keeps failing

You write a perfect budget on the first day of the month. By the tenth, you have abandoned it. A useful budget is one you can live with, not one that looks impressive on paper.

## 11. The Money Chronicles: everyone was buying it

She invested because people in her group chat said they were making money. Other people's excitement is not an investment strategy.

## 12. What is your money supposed to do this year?

You say you want to be better with money, but better can mean almost anything. Give your money a job you can actually describe.

## 13. Do not ignore the cost of borrowing

A loan repayment looks manageable until you add up every fee and the total amount due. A loan should solve a problem, not quietly create a bigger one.

## 14. What happens if your income stops for a while?

It is an uncomfortable question, especially when everything seems fine. Preparing for uncertainty is not being negative. It is being realistic.

## 15. The Money Chronicles: a good year, no assets

He earned well, travelled, bought nice things and enjoyed himself. Enjoying today and planning for tomorrow can happen together.
