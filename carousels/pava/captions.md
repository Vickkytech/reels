# PAVA (@buildwithpava) captions

## 01. You hired staff. Why are you still doing everything?

You have people on your team, but every small decision still lands on your desk. Delegating is not disappearing. It is giving people enough clarity to do the job.

## 02. Your business should not stop when you step out

Imagine taking one afternoon off and returning to twenty missed calls. A good process gives everyone a place to start, even when you are away.

## 03. Busy does not always mean productive

Your team has been working all day. Yet the important things are still waiting. Measure what got finished, not just how tired everyone feels.

## 04. Your customers should not have to ask twice

A customer sends a message on Monday, follows up on Tuesday and gets an answer on Thursday. Customer service is not only about being polite. It is also about being reliable.

## 05. That spreadsheet nobody updates

You created a tracker to make work easier. Two weeks later, everyone has stopped using it. A simple tool people use beats a complicated one they avoid.

## 06. Before you hire another person, check this

You are overwhelmed, so the first thought is to employ someone else. Sometimes you need another employee. Sometimes you need a better way of working.

## 07. Why do the same mistakes keep happening?

An order gets mixed up. Everyone apologises. A month later, it happens again. The point is not to find someone to blame. It is to stop repeating the problem.

## 08. Your business needs a weekly check-in

Everybody is busy, so meetings keep getting postponed. A short useful meeting can save a long stressful conversation later.

## 09. A discount is not always a good idea

Sales are slow, so you cut prices to attract customers. More sales should make the business healthier, not just busier.

## 10. Do you know where your stock goes?

You bought twenty items, sold twelve and can only find six. You cannot manage what you cannot account for.

## 11. Your staff cannot read your mind

You say, "Just handle it the usual way," but the new employee has no idea what that means. Clear instructions save everyone time and frustration.

## 12. Automation will not fix a confusing process

You want an app that automatically handles customer orders. Get the process right before asking technology to speed it up.

## 13. Who checks that payments actually came in?

Someone sends a payment screenshot, and the order moves immediately. Good controls are not about mistrusting customers. They protect the business.

## 14. Your numbers are trying to tell you something

Revenue looks good, but the business account is always running low. Do not wait until payday to discover there is a cash problem.

## 15. Inside the Operations Room: a fresh-produce lesson

Think about a fresh-produce business like Yolafresh. A delay is not just a late delivery. The lesson is simple: operations are part of the customer experience, not just back-office work.
